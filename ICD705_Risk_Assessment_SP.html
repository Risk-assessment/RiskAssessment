Purpose:

The purpose of this risk assessment is to establish standardized procedures for completing an ICD-705 preconstruction risk assessment as part of the concept approval process. The risk assessment results are intended to be utilized when identifying the most appropriate methods to safeguard national security information and comply with ICD-705 requirements for risk management and SCIF accreditation. The results of the risk assessment drive security design & accreditation decisions, from site selection to facility hardening and technical countermeasures, and serves as the foundation for the project's accreditation process. This risk assessment provides the Security Official (SO) and Site Security Manager (SSM) with a preconstruction tool for identifying risks associated with the specific project and characteristics unique to the facility end user/organization. The primary individual purposes of this risk assessment are to:

  1. Identify and Prioritize Assets Requiring Protection
  2. Identify Threats and Adversaries
  3. Identify Vulnerabilities in the Proposed Site & Design
  4. Evaluate Consequences & Impacts
  5. Establish Security Requirements & Design Criteria to Respond to Risks
  6. Support Cost-Benefit and Resource Allocation Decisions
  7. Provide Formal Documentation of Risk Assessments to Assist Future Inspections

The results of this risk assessment will allow the Accrediting Official (AO) and/or Certified TEMPEST Technical Authority (CTTA) to identify countermeasures and acceptable mitigations based on the information provided.

Scope:

The ICD-705 Facility Risk Assessment is an essential process for identifying, evaluating, and mitigating risks associated with protecting U.S. national security. An accurate risk assessment is a mission-critical requirement that ensures national security, compliance, and operational continuity for a SCIF. It is a critical element for accurate SCIF Planning, Design, Construction, and Accreditation. This ICD-705 Facility Risk Assessment may be used by any SCIF project in support of any AO within any Intelligence Community (IC) Element, unless otherwise directed by the AO. The AO may also require additional risk assessments and/or information related to Analytical Risk Management Framework as outlined in references I-IV.

This ICD-705 Facility Risk Assessment should be submitted along with the Concept Approval Request (CAR) to the AO as part of the SCIF sponsorship process. Failure to submit the risk assessment with the CAR and SCIF sponsorship package may result in inadequate requirements being identified, facility design deficiencies, and/or overall adverse impacts to facility accreditation and the protection of US national security.

General:

Completing this risk assessment is intended to ensure compliance with all listed references within this risk assessment. Analytical Risk Management is a critical aspect to determine the most efficient countermeasures required for physical and technical security when planning, designing, construction, and accrediting a SCIF. The AO, CTTA, and/or facility end user may require additional tasks when completing the risk assessment. Additional references and requirements may also apply based upon the project specific AO, CTTA, IC Element, Special Access Program (SAP) Central Office (SAPCO), and end user/organization.

The person(s) completing this risk assessment should consider the following throughout the risk assessment process:

  1. Before completing this risk assessment, complete the ICD-705 Facility Risk Assessment Questionnaire (FRAQ), enclosure 1. The questions on the FRAQ and the identified risk ratings produced by the FRAQ are intended to support the explanations within this risk assessment and identified/required countermeasures.
    1. Since the risk assessment itself is a subjective evaluation, care should be taken to ensure all stakeholders are consulted to develop a tenable description of the threat situation and the resultant risk categorization. Consult the SCIF SO, AO, CTTA, Cognizant Security Authority (CSA), counterintelligence (CI) office, and/or organization executive, when questions exist but cannot be answered by the person(s) completing this risk assessment.
    2. Once approved by the AO, the completed risk assessment must be maintained by the SCIF SO and SSM, or designated information security specialist, throughout construction and included in the final accreditation package. The risk assessment must be available for review upon request by the AO during a SCIF inspection.
    3. The risk assessment should be routinely updated throughout the lifecycle of a SCIF, both throughout construction and after accreditation. Identified increases to risk may require additional countermeasures to be implemented at any point throughout the SCIF lifecycle.

Guide for Completing the ICD-705 Facility Risk Assessment:

1. SECTION 1 – GENERAL INFORMATION:

For sections a-d, provide applicable information. Section e: primary person completing assessment. Section f: facility location. Section g: full description. Section h: renovation history. Section i: overhead images and diagrams.

2. SECTION 2 – ASSETS:

Identify and classify all assets requiring protection: tangible (equipment, materials, people) and intangible (data, information). Be comprehensive.

3. SECTION 3 – THREATS:

Describe threats using authoritative assessments (SETL for OCONUS, NSA IA or CI assessments for CONUS). Consider human, technical, environmental, and natural threats.

4. SECTION 4 – VULNERABILITIES:

Analyze vulnerabilities after identifying assets and threats. Conduct physical site visits. Consider physical, procedural, technical, and operational vulnerabilities.

5. SECTION 5 – RISK ASSESSMENT SUMMARY:

Transfer calculated risk ratings from the FRAQ. Provide detailed analysis explaining which factors contributed most to risk. Recommend specific countermeasures and describe residual risks.

6. SECTION 6 – ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:

Include all additional information ensuring the AO has full understanding of risks. Use for overflow or information not covered elsewhere.
